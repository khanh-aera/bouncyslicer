How to use this

Paste your printer's start/end G-code here for bed leveling, purge lines, and other printer-specific routines. The defaults work for basic printers but your slicer's version will be better.

Variables

Use these placeholders — they get replaced with your settings:
{bed_temp} {hotend_temp} {bed_x} {bed_y} {travel_speed} {nozzle_diameter} {filament_diameter}

Where to find it

  • PrusaSlicer / OrcaSlicer — Printer Settings > Custom G-code. Copy "Start G-code" and "End G-code". Replace temperature placeholders: [first_layer_temperature] → {hotend_temp}, [first_layer_bed_temperature] → {bed_temp}
  • Cura — Preferences > Printers > Machine Settings. Copy "Start G-code" and "End G-code". Replace: {material_print_temperature} → {hotend_temp}, {material_bed_temperature} → {bed_temp}
  • Bambu Studio — Printer tab > Machine G-code. Same as PrusaSlicer placeholders.

Common additions

  • G29 — auto bed leveling (add after G28)
  • BED_MESH_CALIBRATE — Klipper mesh leveling
  • PRINT_START / PRINT_END — Klipper macros
  • Purge line — usually a few G1 moves near bed edge
